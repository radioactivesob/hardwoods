How much did she play?

New in My Kid: tap ON THE BENCH when she checks in and it flips to ON THE
FLOOR with a running clock; tap again when she comes out. Her card shows
playing time as a share of the game — "46% · 18:42" — and the season chart
gets a minutes trend. Forget to tap out at the buzzer? Ending the game
closes it for you.

Team Stats, after its first real weekend

Players are in jersey order on the strip and the box score, so it reads
against the coach's sheet. A chip turns amber at three fouls and red at
four. Select a player and the shooting tiles show her line — "4/9 · 44%" —
so you can see hot or cold at a glance. Someone who didn't show comes off
the strip when you mark her out.

Rosters that just work

Team Stats now has its own roster screen. Name the team, add players, done —
it saves as you type, no button to remember. A girl shows up who isn't on
the list? Tap + ADD on the strip mid-game.

Fix it afterwards

Recorded a line under the wrong name? Long-press the game in Team Seasons,
choose Edit Stats, and move it to whoever it should have been. Adjust any
count there too. Forgot the opponent? That's on the same menu.

Send a team game to another phone

SHARE STATS on the box score sends the game as data. The other phone adds it
to Team Seasons — and offers each player's line to her My Kid profile if she
has one there. Sending the same game twice won't create duplicates.

As always: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone unless
you choose to send it.
