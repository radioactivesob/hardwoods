Back up your kid's season

Hardwoods keeps everything on your phone and nothing in the cloud. That's what
keeps it private — but it also means deleting the app would take the season with
it. Now it doesn't have to. Open your kid's profile, tap BACK UP EVERYTHING, and
send the file to yourself: games, training sessions, and her settings, all in
one. Open that file on any phone and it all comes back. Restoring skips anything
already there, so an older backup fills the gaps and leaves newer games alone.

Send the coach the whole season

Team Seasons has a SHARE SEASON button. A coach with Hardwoods opens that one
file and gets everything — the record, every box score, and the player season
averages table — instead of a screenshot he can't dig into. Send him an updated
file whenever you like; games he already has are skipped. Sharing a single game
still works too, straight from its box score.

Turnovers and blocks

Team Stats now tracks up to twelve stats, so you can add turnovers and blocks
without giving anything up. Both get their own column in the season averages —
TO/G and BPG — when your team tracks them. Worth switching on before a game
rather than after: a stat you didn't track can't be filled in later.

Smaller things

The end-of-game prompt in Team Stats already knows your score — it's been
counting all game — so it fills it in and puts the cursor on the opponent's.
And opening a shared game file no longer leaves a stray screen behind when you
back out of it.

As always: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone unless
you choose to send it.
