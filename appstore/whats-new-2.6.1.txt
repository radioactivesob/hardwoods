Send games between phones

If someone else was sitting with your kid's game — a spouse, a grandparent, the
other parent at an away tournament — they can now send those games straight to
your copy of Hardwoods. Pick the games, share them by AirDrop, text, or email,
and they land in your kid's profile with her number, color, and stat set intact.
Sharing the same games twice won't create duplicates.

Add a game by hand

Missed one, or tracked it on paper? Enter it yourself: date, opponent, an
optional final score, and whichever stats you follow. Hand-entered games show up
everywhere tracked ones do.

Share the numbers, not just a picture

The share card can now send the stats themselves, so another phone can add them
to your kid's profile — right alongside the image you'd text to family.

Large text is properly supported

If you use bigger text on your iPhone, screens no longer break. Labels and
buttons scale to fit instead of overlapping or running off the edge.

Simple Scorebook works upright

No need to rotate the phone. Held upright, you get one team across the top and
one across the bottom. Turn it sideways and the wide layout is still there.

Faster, and lighter on memory

Hardwoods is built on the newest React Native, which clears up a memory problem
that could slow things down over a long game or practice.

As always: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone unless you
choose to send it.
