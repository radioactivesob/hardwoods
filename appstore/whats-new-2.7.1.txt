Track the whole team

New: Team Stats. Every player on your roster on one screen, one tap per stat —
no opponent, no official book. Pick a player, tap what she did, done. When the
game ends you get a box score you can text to the coach, and any player with a
My Kid profile on your phone gets her line saved there too.

Stat tracking, front and center

The home screen now leads with My Kid and Team Stats, because that's what most
of you open Hardwoods for. The scorebooks are still there, right below.

Out tonight

A player who didn't show doesn't have to be deleted anymore. Mark her OUT in
Team Setup and she stays on the roster, skips tonight's pickers, and is back
next week with one tap.

The Full Scorebook works upright

Team A above Team B when you hold the phone in portrait, with a toolbar you can
actually read. Turn it sideways and the wide layout is still there.

Small things you asked for

Forgot the opponent's name? Long-press the game to add it after the fact. Rename
or renumber a profile from its menu. The Full Scorebook now offers to save your
kid's stats to her profile when the game ends. And roster names match profiles
by first name and jersey number, so "Evelyn Cooper" on the roster finds "Evelyn"
on the phone.

As always: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone unless you
choose to send it.
